Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

General Questions

  1. What programs does Apex Crest University offer?

    Apex Crest University offers a wide range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs across faculties such as Science, Engineering, Arts, and Management Sciences.

  2. Where is the university located?

    The university is located at Plot 45, Education Avenue, Abuja, Nigeria.

  3. Is the university accredited?

    Yes, Apex Crest University is fully accredited by the National Universities Commission (NUC).

Admission Questions

  1. How do I apply for admission?

    Applicants are required to complete the online application form, upload necessary documents, and submit their application through the official university portal.

  2. What are the admission requirements?

    Applicants must have at least five (5) O'Level credits including English and Mathematics, a valid UTME score, and must participate in the screening exercise.

  3. When does admission start?

    Admissions typically begin early in the year for the next academic session. Applicants are advised to check the website regularly for updates.

Fees & Scholarships

  1. Are there scholarship opportunities?

    Yes, the university offers merit-based and need-based scholarships to qualified students.

  2. How can I pay my fees?

    All payments must be made through official university-approved channels. Details are provided upon admission.

Student Life

  1. Is accommodation available?

    Yes, the university provides on-campus accommodation for students in a safe and conducive environment.

  2. Are there extracurricular activities?

    Students can participate in various clubs, sports, and social activities that support personal development.

Support & Contact

  1. How can I contact the university?

    You can reach us via email at info@apexcrestuniversity.edu.ng or call +234 800 123 4567.

  2. How long does it take to get a response after applying?

    Applicants will typically receive feedback within a few weeks after the screening process.

About Apex Crest University
