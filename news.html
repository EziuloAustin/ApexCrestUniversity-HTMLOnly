News and Announcements

Admission for 2026/2027 Academic Session Now Open

Apex Crest University hereby announces the commencement of its admission process for the 2026/2027 academic session. Applications are invited from suitably qualified candidates who are passionate about academic excellence and personal development.

Prospective students are encouraged to apply for admission into various undergraduate programs across faculties including Science, Engineering, Arts, and Management Sciences. The university offers a wide range of courses designed to meet global standards and equip students with practical and theoretical knowledge.

Applicants must ensure they meet the minimum admission requirements, including the required OLevel credits and a valid entrance examination score. All applications must be completed online, and candidates are required to upload necessary documents such as academic results and identification.

The admission process is strictly merit-based, and only candidates who successfully meet the criteria will be considered for screening and final selection.

Interested applicants are advised to submit their applications early, as late submissions may not be considered. For further details on the application process, candidates should refer to the admission guidelines provided on the university platform.

Resumption for 2026/2027 Academic Session

The management of Apex Crest University wishes to inform all students that academic activities for the new semester will commence on September 15th, 2026.

Both new and returning students are expected to resume on campus within the stipulated period to begin registration and academic engagements. Late resumption may affect course registration and participation in early academic activities.

Students are advised to complete all necessary registration processes, including course registration and accommodation arrangements, before the commencement of lectures. Academic lectures will begin immediately after the resumption period, and students are expected to attend all scheduled classes.

The university remains committed to providing a conducive learning environment and encourages all students to prepare adequately for the academic session.

Scholarship Program for Students

Apex Crest University is pleased to announce the availability of scholarship opportunities for the 2026/2027 academic session. These scholarships are aimed at supporting academically outstanding students as well as those facing financial challenges.

The scholarship program is designed to promote excellence, reward hard work, and ensure that deserving students have access to quality education regardless of financial constraints. Scholarships may cover partial or full tuition fees depending on the category and eligibility.

Interested students are required to meet specific criteria, including strong academic performance and good character. Applicants may also be required to provide supporting documents and undergo a screening process.

The university encourages all eligible students to take advantage of this opportunity. Further information regarding application procedures and deadlines will be communicated through official channels.

Orientation Program for Newly Admitted Students

Apex Crest University will organize an orientation program for all newly admitted students to officially welcome them into the academic community. The program is scheduled to hold on October 5th, 2026.

The orientation will provide students with essential information about university policies, academic expectations, available resources, and campus life. Students will also have the opportunity to interact with faculty members and administrative staff.

Attendance is mandatory for all new students, as it plays a crucial role in helping them adapt to the university environment.

Launch of New Engineering Laboratories

The university has recently commissioned new state-of-the-art engineering laboratories to enhance practical learning and research capabilities. These facilities are equipped with modern tools and equipment designed to meet industry standards.

This development reflects the institution's commitment to improving the quality of education and providing students with hands-on experience in their respective fields.

Students in engineering and related disciplines are expected to benefit greatly from these upgraded facilities.

Academic Excellence Award Ceremony

Apex Crest University recently held its annual Academic Excellence Award Ceremony to recognize outstanding students for their academic achievements.

Students who demonstrated exceptional performance across various faculties were honored with awards and certificates. The event also highlighted the importance of dedication, discipline, and hard work.

The university remains committed to encouraging academic excellence among its students.

Public Notice on Payment Procedures

The university management wishes to remind all students and parents that all payments must be made through officially approved channels.

Apex Crest University does not authorize any individual or third party to collect fees on its behalf. The institution will not be held responsible for any payments made outside its official system.

Students are strongly advised to verify payment details before making any transactions.
