Admission Overview

Apex Crest University welcomes qualified candidates who are passionate about learning and personal development.

Admission Requirements

Applicants must have the following requirements

  • Minimum of five (5) O'Level credits including English and Mathematics
  • Valid UTME score
  • Participation in screening exercise

How to Apply
