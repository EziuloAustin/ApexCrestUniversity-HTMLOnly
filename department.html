Faculties and Departments

Faculties with Departments

  1. Faculty of Science - Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry
  2. Faculty of Engineering - Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering and Electrical Engineering
  3. Faculty of Arts - English Language, History and Interantional Studies and Philosophy
  4. Faculty of Management Science - Accounting, Business Administration and Banking and Finance
  5. Faculty of Humanities - Religious Studies, Linguistics and Theatre Arts
  6. Faculty of Agriculture - Agricultural Economics, Animal Science and Crop Science

Faculty of Science

The Faculty of Science explores the natural world through observation, experimentation, and rigorous analysis to uncover the laws governing the universe. It prepares students to drive innovation and solve complex global challenges using the scientific method and empirical research.

  • Computer Science - Design software and systems to solve problems through coding, algorithms, and computational theory.
  • Mathematics - Study patterns, quantities, and space to provide the logical framework for science and technology.
  • Physics - Explore the laws of matter and energy to understand how the universe functions.
  • Chemistry - Investigate the properties and reactions of substances to develop new materials and medicines.

Faculty of Engineering

The Faculty of Engineering focuses on applying scientific and mathematical principles to design, build, and maintain innovative structures and systems. It equips students with technical expertise to solve complex problems and advance global technology and infrastructure.

  • Civil Engineering - Design and build essential infrastructure like roads, bridges, and buildings to support modern society.
  • Mechanical Engineering - Develop and maintain physical machines and thermal systems ranging from small tools to large engines.
  • Electrical Engineering - Study and apply electricity, electronics, and electromagnetism to power devices and complex communication systems.

Faculty of Arts

The Faculty of Arts explores the depth of human expression and culture through the study of language, history, and creative thought. It equips students with critical thinking and communication skills to interpret and shape the world around them.

  • English Language - Master the mechanics of language while analyzing literature to sharpen your writing.
  • History & International Studies - Explore past events and political systems to understand how nations interact globally.
  • Philosophy - Tackle deep questions about existence and ethics using logic and critical thinking.

Faculty of Management Science

The Faculty of Management Science prepares students to lead and innovate by combining analytical decision-making with strategic organizational leadership. It focuses on mastering the efficient use of resources and technology to drive growth in the modern global economy.

  • Accounting - Track and analyze financial records to ensure accuracy and legal compliance for organizations.
  • Business Administration - Learn to manage people and processes to optimize the overall performance of a company.
  • Banking and Finance - Study the management of money and assets within financial institutions and global markets.

Faculty of Humanities

The Faculty of Humanities focuses on the study of human culture, history, and communication to better understand our shared social experience. It fosters deep critical thinking and creative expression through the lens of human values and achievements.

  • Religious Studies - Examines the beliefs, practices, and histories of world religions and their impact on global culture.
  • Linguistics - Analyzes the scientific structure of language, how it is formed, and how it evolves across different societies.
  • Theatre Arts - Explores the world of performance, drama, and technical stagecraft to communicate powerful stories and ideas.

Faculty of Agriculture

The Faculty of Agriculture focuses on the scientific and economic principles of food production, natural resource management, and sustainable farming. It bridges the gap between traditional farming and modern technology to ensure global food security and environmental health.

  • Agricultural Economics - Apply economic theories and business management principles to optimize the production, distribution, and marketing of agricultural products.
  • Animal Science - Study the biology, breeding, and management of livestock to improve the efficiency and welfare of animal-based food production systems.
  • Crop Science - Explore plant genetics, soil management, and pest control to develop high-yielding and resilient crops for sustainable agriculture.
